How recruitment works

Notice, application file, written test, oral test, interview.

Recruitment for the next school year is normally announced in the spring, on this website and on the notice boards at the school and its branches, with applications due in April–June.

  1. Read the notice

    The recruitment notice lists the positions, qualifications and deadline. Only applications that meet the criteria are shortlisted.

  2. Submit your file

    Deliver a complete application in a sealed envelope addressed to the Director, to the school reception or the Administration & HR office (working days, 7:30am–3:45pm). Documents are not returned.

  3. Written test

    Shortlisted candidates sit a written test in their subject and in English.

  4. Oral test and interview

    Candidates who pass the written test are invited to an oral test, and then to a final interview.

Your application file

  • A motivation letter addressed to the Director of Discovery School.
  • A recommendation letter signed by the pastor of your church.
  • A detailed, updated, dated and signed CV.
  • A certified copy of your diploma(s).
  • A certificate of non-indebtedness for any government or institutional scholarship (proof of repayment, if applicable).
  • A photocopy of a valid national ID card or passport.
  • A current criminal-record extract (casier judiciaire).

Who we are looking for

  • A born-again Christian and member of an evangelical church, with a deep faith in Christ and outstanding moral integrity.
  • Burundian nationality.
  • The required diploma for the position, recognised by the Burundian government.
  • Able to teach and communicate effectively in English (French for some positions).
  • Experience in education or youth work, and confidence with Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Google Classroom, are assets.
  • Pass the recruitment test and be available for the contract period.

What we offer

  • Health insurance.
  • Reduced-cost education at Discovery for your own children.
  • A staff savings and lending programme.
  • High-quality on-the-job training through internal courses and visiting seminars.
  • A staff family that shares daily devotions and prayer, and supports one another through life's events.
