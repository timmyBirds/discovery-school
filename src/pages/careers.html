How the exam works

  • Register your interest by contacting the school before the exam date.
  • Bring your diplomas, teaching qualification and identification.
  • The exam assesses subject knowledge and language (English or French, according to the position).
  • Successful candidates are invited to interview and a demonstration lesson.

Who we are looking for

  • A professing Christian with a deep faith in Christ and outstanding moral integrity, committed to the school's Christian principles.
  • Regular church attendance, with a reference letter from your pastor.
  • Burundian government teaching qualifications for the level you will teach.
  • Fluency in English or French, depending on the teaching assignment.

What we offer

  • Health insurance.
  • Reduced-cost education at Discovery for your own children.
  • A staff savings and lending programme.
  • High-quality on-the-job training through internal courses and visiting seminars.
  • A staff family that shares daily devotions and prayer, and supports one another through life's events.
