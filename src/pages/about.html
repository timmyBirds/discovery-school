Vision

Rooted in Christ. Ready for Tomorrow.

Mission

To plant seeds for Burundi's future by providing a high-quality, sustainable, critical-thinking-based Christian education.

The best preparation we can give students for whatever they may face in the future is to lead them in a relationship with Christ. From that root, we seek to:

  • offer an English-language education that develops language fluency, technological ability and cross-cultural understanding;
  • develop well-rounded critical thinkers with a Christian worldview, equipped as problem-solvers for Burundi and beyond;
  • challenge every learner to grow spiritually and to develop Christ-like character.
“So then, just as you received Christ Jesus as Lord, continue to live your lives in him, rooted and built up in him, strengthened in the faith as you were taught, and overflowing with thankfulness.”Colossians 2:6–7 — our school verse

The image of a tree runs through Scripture — the one who trusts in the Lord is “like a tree planted by the water” (Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalm 1:3), and Jesus is the vine in whom we bear fruit (John 15:5).

Annual verse. Each year we also choose a theme for the school. This year's is Job 37:14b — “Stop and consider God's wonders.”

Who we are

Authentically Burundian. Internationally minded.

Truly Burundian

Our students are Burundian children and our teachers are overwhelmingly Burundian. Children learn to read first in their mother tongue, study Kirundi throughout primary, and learn Burundian history, geography and government. We want graduates who love their country and stay to build it.

An international standard

English is the language of instruction from the first years of primary. We use an international curriculum, our students sit the IOWA test and the SAT, and the school is accredited by the National Association of Private Schools. Graduates are prepared for university in Burundi or anywhere in the world.

A Christian heart

Every teacher is a professing Christian. Days begin with devotions and prayer, Bible is taught in every grade, a Biblical worldview shapes every subject, and our chaplain prays with and counsels each student. Our students are known and loved.

Affordable, on purpose

We are convinced that no other school in Bujumbura offers an education of this quality at fees this accessible. Keeping Discovery affordable for ordinary Burundian families is part of the mission, made possible by careful stewardship and by partners around the world.

Accreditation

Accredited by the National Association of Private Schools.

Discovery School holds international accreditation through NAPS, which reviews our curriculum, teaching, governance and student outcomes against an international standard.

Accreditation matters for families because it is an independent check on quality — and because Discovery graduates who reach the required standard on their final international high-school exams receive equivalence to the Burundian government diploma (Diplôme d'État), opening the door to universities at home and abroad.

Our staff

A family of more than 150 teachers and staff.

Discovery's teachers, support staff and administrators are organised by nursery & preschool, primary, secondary and supporting teams.

Every full-time member of staff is a professing Christian who meets Burundian government teaching qualifications, attends church regularly, and is fluent in English or French according to their teaching assignment. Staff receive high-quality on-the-job training through internal courses and visiting seminars, and we describe ourselves honestly as a family — sharing daily devotions and prayer, and supporting one another through life's events.
