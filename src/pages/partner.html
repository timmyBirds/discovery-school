Long-term impact

Why a school? Why this one?

Burundi is one of the youngest and poorest countries in the world. What its next generation of leaders, teachers, doctors, pastors and entrepreneurs believe — and how well they can think — will shape the nation for decades.

Rooted in Christ

Every Discovery student hears the gospel daily, is prayed for by name, and learns to see every subject through a Biblical worldview. We are raising up young people of Christian character who love God and love their country.

Ready for tomorrow

Graduates are trilingual critical thinkers with internationally recognised qualifications. Since our first graduating class, alumni have gone on to universities in Burundi and across Africa, Europe, Asia and North America — and many come home to serve.

Sustainable and affordable

Discovery is a Burundian school with Burundian staff, run on Burundian fees. Partner gifts do not replace that — they multiply it: funding facilities, equipment and training that fees alone could never cover, while keeping fees within reach of ordinary families.

Build

Building projects.

Discovery has grown one grade at a time — building classrooms as each new class arrived — until we reached a complete high school. Now we are building the specialty facilities a full secondary school needs.

  • Science laboratories — properly equipped labs for biology, chemistry and physics.
  • Music room — a home for the choir, recorder classes and instruments.
  • Auditorium furnishings — for chapel, the Christmas production and assemblies.

Please pray for God's continued provision, and contact us if you would like to fund a building project or furnish a classroom.

Visit & volunteer

Come and see how God can use your gifts.

Contact us any time to arrange a tour. Individuals and teams are welcome for a week, a month, or longer.

Short-term (under a month)

Teams and individuals have led Bible clubs, run skateboard clinics, taught playground games like four square and kickball, built playgrounds, taught cooking, art and music, given Bible lessons, led teacher seminars, and helped children with reading.

Long-term (a month or more)

Longer stays allow deeper involvement: work alongside our teachers, accountants or librarians, learning from one another's ideas and methods.

Travel information

We provide guidance on getting to Burundi, preparation, what to pack, and expected expenses for volunteers. Contact us to start planning a visit.
