Admissions process

Four steps to enrol.

Applications are made in person at the school, because every child takes a placement test in English and mathematics. We do not offer online applications.

Admissions are open for all grades from crèche to Grade 13, subject to available places. New students are welcomed for the start of the school year in September; enquire about mid-year places.

Book a visit Call +257 22 23 20 59

  1. Visit the school

    Come to the school office in Gihosha during office hours. We would love to show you the campus and answer your questions. No appointment is needed, but you are welcome to contact us first.

  2. Bring the documents

    Your child's birth certificate, last year's report card, and the application fee (30,000–40,000 FBu depending on the grade). You will receive a registration form to complete at the school.

  3. Placement test

    Every applicant takes a short placement test in English and mathematics so we can place them in the right grade and give the right support from day one.

  4. Enrol and pay

    Once a place is confirmed, pay the registration and first-term fees at the office or by mobile money, collect the uniform and book list, and get ready for the first day.

Requirements

What you will need.

  • Birth certificate of the child.
  • Previous year's report card (for students transferring from another school).
  • Application fee of 30,000–40,000 FBu, depending on grade level.
  • Placement test in English and mathematics, taken at the school.
  • Registration form, provided and completed at the school.

Children entering nursery or preschool are placed by age; older students are placed according to their previous schooling and the placement test.

Not sure which grade?

Burundi's primary grades are P1–P6, followed by secondary G7–G13. Discovery also has a nursery (crèche) and two preschool years (M1, M2). If your child is transferring from a French-medium school, the placement test helps us make sure English support is in place.

How to pay

Pay at the office, by bank, or by mobile money.

Fees can be paid in cash at the school office, by bank transfer, or from your phone with EcoCash or Lumicash. Always include the student's ID number (for example 12/0031) or the student's name.

Need help?

For help with EcoCash or Lumicash payments, call the accounts office on 22 23 16 55, or bring your payment to the office.

EcoCash — biller code 03030

  1. Dial *444# and enter your PIN.
  2. Choose 4 — Payer la facture.
  3. Enter the biller code 03030.
  4. Enter the amount.
  5. Account number: the student's ID (e.g. 12/0031) or, if you don't have it, the student's name (e.g. Iranzi/Queen).
  6. Enter 1 to confirm.

Lumicash — merchant code 444555

  1. Dial *163#.
  2. Choose 5 — Payer le marchand, then 1.
  3. Enter the merchant code 444555.
  4. Enter the amount.
  5. Payment description, e.g. Minerval pour Iradukunda Armel.
  6. Enter your PIN, then 1 to confirm.

Questions families ask

Good to know.

My child does not speak English yet. Can they still join?

Yes. Many children join Discovery with little English. The placement test tells us where support is needed, and English immersion from primary means children pick it up remarkably quickly — while continuing to learn in Kirundi and French.

What are the school hours?

All students begin at 7:30am. Nursery and preschool finish at 11:45; primary and secondary hours increase by grade until the full day (to 3:35/3:45pm, Monday–Thursday) from P5. See the daily schedules.

Is lunch provided?

Lunch is served at the school Monday to Thursday for all students required to stay for afternoon classes.

Is there a school bus?

Yes — optional bus transport is available, charged per term according to the pick-up zone. Ask the office for current routes.

Does my family need to be Christian?

No. Discovery is a Christian school and every student takes part in Bible lessons, prayer and chapel, but we welcome families from any background who are happy for their child to be educated in a Christian environment.

Can I apply online?

No — applications are made in person because every child takes a placement test at the school. You are welcome to contact us with questions before you visit.
