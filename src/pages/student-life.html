Student life

A full life of faith, learning and discovery.

Discovery students grow through worship, sport, music and art, Friday clubs, field trips, and leading one another — under mango trees on a campus that feels like home.

Spiritual life

Rooted in Christ, every day.

Our chaplain takes time to pray with and counsel each student, and a Biblical worldview is taught in every subject.

  • Preschool & primary: a daily Bible lesson and prayer time.
  • Middle school: Bible class twice a week.
  • High school: Bible class every day on alternate weeks.
  • Chapel: every class has chapel once a month — worship led by the choir, then a short message.
  • Thanksgiving & Praise Chapel: once a term, primary and secondary gather in larger groups to give thanks together.

Secondary students can also join the Bible Club, the Evangelism Team, and the Chapel Leadership Team.

“…rooted and built up in him, strengthened in the faith as you were taught, and overflowing with thankfulness.”Colossians 2:7

Athletics

Teamwork, servant leadership, sportsmanship, discipline and determination.

We compete because competition — done well — teaches selflessness, sacrifice and perseverance. Discovery fields teams in football, basketball, swimming and track & field.

Interschool competition

Our teams compete against other Bujumbura schools in football, basketball, swimming and track events.

Intramurals

Class-versus-class football tournaments every term with trophies for the boys' and girls' winners — and the annual secondary King of the Jungle, where teams battle in volleyball, basketball, football and more.

Coaches

Coach Enock played first-division football in Burundi and for national youth teams. Coach Aloys, our PE teacher, holds ITF-recognised coaching credentials across athletics, handball, football, judo, rugby, swimming and tennis.

Fine arts

Discovering God-given talents.

A Christ-centred arts curriculum that inspires students to discover and nurture their gifts, develop an appreciation for the arts, and use them to glorify God.

The youngest learners create every day. Primary students have short art and music classes each week (or longer classes on alternate weeks) — art focused on elements and technique; music exploring melody, harmony and rhythm with instruments from around the world. Secondary students learn to read music, play the recorder, and may join the school choir, which also leads worship in chapel.

Every December the whole school takes part in a Christmas production — singing, traditional dancing and a theatrical play with costumes and memorised lines, performed twice so every grade can take part.

Art

Elements, technique, and creative projects — t-shirt design, model homes, film-making, carpentry.

Music

Melody, harmony and rhythm; recorder and reading music in secondary; the school choir.

Drama & dance

The annual Christmas production with traditional dancing and theatre.

Ingenuity

Students with a creative idea are encouraged to make it — in class, art club or science club.

Friday clubs & media

Three Fridays a month, students choose.

Secondary students attend a club of their choice three Fridays each month; the fourth Friday is spent in chapel.

Clubs include chess, sewing, art, science and computing, and change with student interest. Students can also join the media team, creating school social-media posts and helping produce the Senior Magazine and Yearbook — working in English, in a real publishing workflow.

Field trips

Students often explore nature on walks around the larger CEEM compound where the school is located, and have visited expos, the Musée Vivant and other nearby sites. Virtual field trips with VR cardboard glasses bring further-away places into the classroom.

Student government

Students elect a Student Body President and cabinet who strengthen communication between students and staff, and develop ideas for improving student life. Leadership is learned by leading.
