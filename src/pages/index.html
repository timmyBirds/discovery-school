Our foundation

A truly Burundian school, with an international standard of learning.

Discovery School is a Christian preschool, primary and secondary school in Gihosha, Bujumbura, on a green campus of mango trees and shaded courtyards.

Our students are Burundian children, taught by Burundian teachers, who learn Burundian history, geography and government in their own languages — and learn through English immersion with an international curriculum, international testing, and internationally-recognised accreditation. Every child is known, loved, challenged, and equipped to grow in faith, character, knowledge and practical skill.

Unmatched value

An education of this quality, at a price Bujumbura families can afford.

Ready for tomorrow

What a Discovery graduate carries into the world.

Our aim is simple to say and demanding to deliver: graduates who are trilingual critical thinkers with a Biblical worldview, ready for university and for life in Burundi, the region and the world.

Trilingual

Fluent in English through immersion from preschool, confident in French, and grounded in Kirundi — with Swahili as a fourth language in primary.

Critical thinkers

Taught to question, investigate and solve problems — not just memorise. Students sit the IOWA test at the end of middle school and the SAT at graduation.

A Biblical worldview

Faith integrated into every subject, so that graduates go out as people of Christian character who love God and love Burundi.

Since our first graduating class, Discovery alumni have gone on to universities in Burundi and abroad — across Africa, Europe, Asia and North America. Graduates who reach the required standard on their final international exams receive equivalence to the Burundian Diplôme d'État.

Heads, hearts and hands

Learning by doing.

Science labs, sewing machines, computer labs, sports fields, choir, art, chess and Friday clubs — because children learn by seeing, hearing, making and moving.
