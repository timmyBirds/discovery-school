Lunch

Lunch is served at the school Monday to Thursday for all students required to stay for afternoon classes. Weekly lunch menus (and the boarding menu) are shared with families.

Class WhatsApp groups

Each class has a WhatsApp group for parents. To join, send a written message to +257 68 93 54 99 with the parent's name, the child's name and the class.

Contact the office

Call +257 22 23 20 59 or email info@discoveryschoolburundi.org. For fee payment help, the accounts office is on 22 23 16 55.

Learning support

In-school lessons are available for students in P1–G9 who are struggling in maths or reading, or who have a learning disability. Speak to your child's teacher or the office.
